I'm a support professional with more than five years in technical support, client success, and SaaS environments. I'm at my best when a confusing symptom turns into a clear cause, and I explain it in plain language so the person on the other end can get back to work.

Right now I'm a Senior Product Support Specialist at Aderant, supporting an eBilling platform used by law firms around the world. Before that I worked in support engineering and customer success, where I led client onboarding, wrote knowledge base articles that reduced ticket volume, and passed structured usability feedback to engineering teams.

I work in Armenian, English, Russian, and Arabic. I also produced, directed, and co-wrote a documentary that was nominated in the Feature-Length Documentaries category at the 29th Annual ARPA International Film Festival. It taught me project management, storytelling, and cross-cultural communication.
